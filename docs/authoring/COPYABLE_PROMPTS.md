```text
production stages, actual tools, demonstrated settings, ordered actions, required

complete official prompts exactly, with their source locations and attribution.

homework answer/asset field. List missing required facts and useful recommendations
separately. Never carry old lesson facts, URLs, products or task counts from a
reference. Check the schedule for an existing ID or future placeholder.

Deliver a filled brief and proposed three-deliverable outline. Do not create or
change the schedule, student pages, Google Doc permissions or official prompts
while doing this source-extraction task.
```
