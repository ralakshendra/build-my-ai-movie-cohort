## Preparation and workflow

- Choose a four-wheel vehicle (car/SUV/sedan); the instructor explicitly set bikes aside because their workflow differs.
- Gather reference images from multiple angles and keep the vehicle colour consistent across the set. Use a three-quarter view as a practical starting reference; use a 3D vehicle model/viewer to capture a needed camera angle when available.
- If the chosen vehicle is repeatedly inaccurate because the model lacks useful reference knowledge or confuses it with a similar design, choose another vehicle or strengthen the references before generating more.
- Plan an advertisement as individual shots. Select the video method for each shot; do not constrain the ideation prompt to one generation method before the shot needs are clear.
- Use start/end frames when both frames describe the same vehicle and scene and the intended change is coherent. Keep the environment and parallax stable. Use another method/reference when the endpoints are unrelated.
- Keep motion prompts short and specific. Check wheel rotation, water and road interaction, camera path and background motion against plausible physics. Add sound effects separately when generated audio is missing or unreliable; use licensed/authorized audio.
